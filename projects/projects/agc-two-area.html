Academic Project

Automatic Generation Control in a Two-Area System

Simulink

Characterized the dynamic inter-dependency between load disturbances, system frequency excursions, and tie-line power deviations in a two-area interconnected power system.

Key contributions

  • Modeled a two-area power system in Simulink, including governor, turbine, and load-frequency control blocks.
  • Analyzed how a load disturbance in one area propagates into frequency deviation and tie-line power flow in the other.
  • Compared system response with and without automatic generation control to evaluate stability improvement.
