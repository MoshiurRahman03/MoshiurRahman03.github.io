1500W Sinusoidal PWM Inverter

PSpice Proteus 555 Timer

Designed and validated a 1500W sinusoidal PWM inverter focused on stable, low-noise power supply design. Used PSpice to optimize switching losses and Proteus for microcontroller integration, ensuring clean PWM generation with a 555 timer.

Key contributions

  • Designed the full inverter circuit and simulated switching behavior in PSpice to minimize switching losses.
  • Generated PWM signals using a 555 timer and verified microcontroller integration in Proteus.
  • Validated output waveform quality and noise performance against target specifications.
